Sales X-Ray 360

A per-store diagnostic report for a network of hundreds of stores: it compares each store with similar stores and shows where the difference comes from.

Every number on this page is fictitious: an invented network of 60 stores, generated by a script with the same calculation rules as the original reports.

Context

A store's numbers were spread across three Power BI models (orders, campaigns and satisfaction) and month-end spreadsheets. Understanding one store meant opening several reports and comparing by hand, usually against the network average, which mixes very different stores.

The brief was a 360° diagnosis that works for every store, can be audited, and uses only the data that exists, making clear what is missing.

What the report answers

  • How is the store doing against similar stores (region, format, type, franchisee or the network's best)?
  • Does the gap come from volume or ticket, and within the ticket, from items, price or delivery fee?
  • On which days and hours do customers order, and where does the store depart from its group?
  • Through which channels and service methods do orders arrive?
  • Which products carry the revenue?
  • Where does operations lose time between order and delivery?
  • Which hypotheses and actions are worth testing, and what could they be worth per week?
  • Do the two sources of the monthly close tell the same story?

How it was built

The project is a PBIP: the model lives in TMDL files and the report in PBIR, all as text. Python scripts write these files from the source tables: one builds the model and measures, another builds the 14 pages with visuals, synced slicers and tooltips.

Sources
orders and operations timings, items sold, campaigns, products, daily budget and the monthly close spreadsheet
Model
about 60 tables and 480 measures, with calculation groups for chart series
Pages
KPIs, trends, variance breakdown, demand, channel and method, ABC curve, product and campaign, operations, hypotheses, actions, gaps, definitions and two source reconciliations
Privacy
no customer name, phone or address enters the model

When someone tweaks the layout by hand in Power BI Desktop, a script compares the saved file with the generated one and stores the differences. The next version reapplies them. It also keeps what was selected in the slicers and the stacking order of visuals.

Which products carry the revenue

The ABC curve sorts items by revenue (or volume) and marks the 80% and 95% cut-offs. It grounds conversations about mix, stock-outs and menu in the store's own data.

When sources disagree

The official sales close came from a finance spreadsheet, while the report read the order system. A selector lets the first three pages use either source, and two pages reconcile the sources by month, day and store.

The close is monthly. To use it in daily or weekly charts, each store-month value is spread across days and channels following the daily base's profile, and the full month adds up exactly to the official number.

The network total almost matches; by day and by store, the differences show up.

Business rules that made a difference

Same storesSSS and SSO only use stores that sold in both periods, comparing the same calendar dates: holiday with holiday, a regular day with 52 weeks earlier.
No budget allocationThe budget has no channel, method, hour or product. With one of those filters on, attainment is left blank and the report says why.
Not enough dataWithout a base, the card states the reason (for example, last year starts before the data) instead of a misleading number.
Configurable groupNetwork, same region, format, type, franchisee, hand-picked stores, or the best or worst stores by a KPI.
Evidence-based hypothesesEach active hypothesis shows the evidence, how to confirm it, where to look in the report, and an action with an estimated weekly gain. Triggers live in a parameters table.

Limits and next steps

  • Product cost and margin depend on a COGS source the model does not have yet.
  • Some pages evaluate many rules against the group and take a few seconds on first load.
  • Next step: connect this report to the financial diagnosis, to see sales and margin for the same store side by side.
